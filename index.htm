Königsberg 1736
Königsberg, Prussia · 1736

Walk the city of seven bridges

In 1736 Leonhard Euler studied a puzzle about this city. The Pregel river splits it into four parts, joined by seven bridges. Can you walk through town and cross every bridge exactly once?

Walk past the cathedral on the Kneiphof, up to the castle, and through streets of half-timbered houses. The tally keeps count of every crossing.
